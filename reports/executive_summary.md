# Executive Summary: European Banking Customer Churn & Retention Strategy

**Target Audience:** C-Suite Banking Executives, Heads of Retail Banking, Risk Officers  
**Dataset Scope:** 10,000 Retail Banking Customers across France, Germany, and Spain  

---

### 1. Executive Brief
Customer attrition across the bank's European retail portfolio stands at **20.37%** (2,037 churned customers out of 10,000), representing **€185.59M** in total balance exposure. 

The empirical analysis reveals acute concentration of churn risk within specific geographic regions, age cohorts, and behavioral segments, offering immediate targets for retention interventions.

---

### 3. Core Strategic Insights

1. **Germany is the Primary Geographic Risk:** German customers churn at double the rate of France (16.15%) and Spain (16.67%), while holding substantially higher deposit balances.
2. **Pre-Retirement & Mature Customers (46–60) Face Maximum Attrition:** With a 51.12% churn rate, this affluent demographic represents the greatest single risk to portfolio balance stability.
3. **The 2-Product Anchor Effect:** Customers with 2 products demonstrate outstanding loyalty (7.58% churn rate), while single-product customers churn at 27.71%.
4. **Inactivity Predicts Liquidity Drainage:** Inactive members account for 63.8% of all churned balance exposure (€118.53M).

---

### 4. Executive Action Plan

1. **Immediate German Market Taskforce:** Conduct competitive benchmarking on deposit rates and service satisfaction in Germany.
2. **Wealth & Mature Segment Retention Campaign:** Deliver customized wealth transition and term-deposit solutions for customers aged 45–60.
3. **Cross-Sell Acceleration to 2 Products:** Establish systematic automated onboarding pathways to convert 1-product customers into 2-product relationships.
4. **Automated Inactive Account Re-Engagement:** Deploy activity triggers and personalized incentives when account activity ceases for $>60$ days.
5. **High-Value Concierge Retention:** Assign relationship managers to high-balance ($> €131.5k$) accounts showing early signs of inactivity.
